## Overview

Building Blocks Nursery School has taught Brampton's children since 1986 — half-day Preschool, Junior Kindergarten and Senior Kindergarten for ages 2½ to 5, licensed by the Ontario Ministry of Education. The site had to do what a nursery school's front door does: feel warm and safe in the first second, then answer a parent's real questions — ratios, fees, subsidy, what a day looks like — without making them call to find out.

The whole site is built around one action: **book a tour**.

## Key Features

- **An identity built from the name**: The logo's alphabet blocks become the site's motif. An animated intro stacks them into the wordmark, and a small interactive toy lets a parent type their child's name and watch it build in blocks — a small moment of delight that makes the school feel like itself.
- **Programs as a decision tool**: Preschool, JK and SK each get their own page, colour and age band, so a parent lands on the one that matches their child instead of reading all three.
- **The money questions, answered up front**: A dedicated fees page covers base and non-base fees, CWELCC enrolment, and Region of Peel subsidy, with the current fee schedule and closure days downloadable as a one-page PDF.
- **Parent self-service**: Handbook, registration and immunization forms, the yearly calendar, events and an FAQ are all reachable without an email to the office.
- **Trust signals where they matter**: Licensing, CWELCC enrolment, subsidy acceptance and "serving Brampton families since 1986" sit directly under the hero, next to the 8:1 ratio and half-day format.
- **Accessible by design**: Skip links, an accessibility statement and a reduced-motion-friendly intro, because a school site has a wider audience than most.

## Technologies Used

- **Next.js (App Router)**: Static generation for every program, policy and parent page, so the site is fast on a phone in a parking lot.
- **Vercel**: Hosting and preview deployments.
- **Structured metadata and sitemap**: Local-business details, address and phone marked up for search and map results.

## Outcome

Every question that used to require a phone call during office hours now has a page: compare the three programs, read the handbook, check subsidy eligibility, download the fee schedule, and book a tour — at any hour, from a phone.
