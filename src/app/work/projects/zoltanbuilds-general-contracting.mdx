## Overview

ZoltanBuilds is a licensed and insured general contractor working across Guelph, Kitchener-Waterloo, Cambridge, Halton Hills and the GTA. Renovation clients don't shop the way software buyers do — they want to know someone real will show up, that the work is insured, and roughly what it costs, in that order. The site is built to answer all three before the homeowner has scrolled.

The promise in the hero is the product: **quoted in 24 hours**.

## Key Features

- **A three-step quote flow**: Name and number, then the job and a few photos, then an estimate within 24 hours. The first step asks for almost nothing, because the cost of asking for too much up front is the lead.
- **A local page per service area**: Guelph, Kitchener-Waterloo, Cambridge, Halton Hills and the GTA each get a page written for that market — Guelph's talks about limestone-foundation century homes in Old University and mid-century side-splits in the South End, not generic "we serve your area" filler.
- **Documented projects**: Project pages carry finished and in-progress photography, including rough-cut timber structure and framing, so the trade work is visible rather than asserted.
- **Proof, not adjectives**: 4.8 on Houzz and 5.0 on Google, licensing and insurance, and 24/7 emergency availability sit in the hero next to the form.
- **Custom navigation without a framework**: The two header dropdowns share a single popup that resizes and slides between them — the Base UI NavigationMenu pattern, rebuilt in plain CSS and JavaScript to keep the site dependency-free.
- **Built to be read by machines too**: `llms.txt`, an `ai-catalog.json` and `.well-known/agent-card.json` publish the business facts — services, service areas, contact, booking — as a source of truth for AI assistants, with explicit notes on what not to infer. Admin and API paths are excluded.

## Technologies Used

- **Static HTML, CSS and JavaScript**: No framework. Every page ships as static HTML, which keeps it fast on rural connections and trivial to host.
- **Vercel**: Hosting on the `zoltanbuilds.ca` domain.
- **Cal.com**: 15-minute consultation booking.
- **Structured data and llms.txt**: Local-business markup for search, plus an agent-readable catalog for conversational AI.

## Challenges and Learnings

The interesting constraint was *restraint*. It would have been easy to reach for a framework and ship a slower site; the navigation menu was the one piece of real interaction complexity, and rebuilding it by hand kept the whole thing static. The second was writing five local pages that are genuinely different — local SEO rewards specificity, and specificity means actually knowing the housing stock in each town.

## Outcome

The site routes a homeowner from "I need a contractor in Guelph" to a photo-backed quote request in two clicks, and gives search engines and AI assistants a canonical, unambiguous set of facts to answer from.
